Scope: admin.qapchaconews.com, every screen of the panel. Visitor mode: Operate. Full spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-panel-admin-design.md.

Audience and job: 3 admins and 2 editors, at a computer in an office by day, publishing news and deciding what leads.

Memorable moment: opening Hoy and knowing at once what needs doing, with one click to fix each item.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard played straight at full craft, as the owner asked: a modern admin in the manner of TailAdmin, built from scratch for a newsroom. It refuses cleverness in navigation and controls, and refuses copying that template's code, assets or brand.

OWN-WORLD: White rail and top bar over a pale grey ground, thin-bordered cards with 16 px radius, accent #465fff on actions and selection, green for rises, red only for errors and danger. Outfit, tabular figures. Soft gradient under area charts. Full light and dark themes, AA in both. QAP logo identifies it.

STORY: The editor opens the panel, sees what needs them and what is being read, fixes it in one click, and writes. They trust it because nothing is lost and nothing happens without them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 264 px white rail with QAP logo and groups Contenido, Comunidad, Análisis, Ajustes, with counters. 64 px top bar: collapse, search with Ctrl K, then at right Nueva nota, theme, notifications, user. Then four figure cards; below, 8 of 12 columns for the reads chart with 12 meses, 30 días, 7 días tabs, and 4 of 12 for Requiere atención; below, Más leídas and Lecturas por sección.

FORM: The category standard, chosen by the owner over the dealt directions, with their reference as the craft bar; seed:c24f2011063b17d4. Signature interaction: Ctrl K search that reaches any note, user or action. Motion grammar: 150-250 ms, state changes only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
